## Overview

This is not even a competition at this point ... this is embarrassing

This is a web application built on top of the Local Machine Learning (LLaMA) platform, specifically designed to facilitate discussions around the topic of AI and machine learning. The application is intended for users who want to engage in open and honest conversations about the potential implications of AI on society. The platform features a unique interface that allows users to anonymously share their thoughts and opinions without fear of judgment or reprisal. This is a crucial aspect of the platform's design, as it acknowledges the sensitive nature of the topics being discussed.

A concrete scenario of someone using this platform is a local university student who is struggling to balance their coursework with their desire to participate in online discussions about AI ethics. They may use the platform to share their thoughts on the topic with a small group of peers, and to receive feedback and support from others who are going through similar experiences. Alternatively, they may use the platform to engage with experts in the field, asking questions and seeking clarification on complex topics.

The application's capabilities include:

* Anonymous sharing of thoughts and opinions
* Support for multiple discussion threads and topics
* Ability to engage with users through comments and direct messages
* Integration with existing social media platforms for easy sharing and tagging

Real-world use cases for this platform include:

* Academic research and collaboration
* Community building and discussion forums
* Online support groups for individuals dealing with AI-related anxiety or stress

```bash

pip install -r requirements.txt
python main.py
```
